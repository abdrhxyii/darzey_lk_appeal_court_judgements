# ⚖️#SriLanka 🇱🇰 Appeal Court Judgements `Dataset`

[https://github.com/abdrhxyii/lk_appeal_court_judgements/tree/data/data/lk_appeal_court_judgements](https://github.com/abdrhxyii/lk_appeal_court_judgements/tree/data/data/lk_appeal_court_judgements)

A Court of Appeal judgment is a higher court ruling that reviews decisions of lower courts, shaping legal precedent and protecting citizens’ rights.

- [**18,521** documents](https://github.com/abdrhxyii/lk_appeal_court_judgements/tree/data/data/lk_appeal_court_judgements) (**16.0 GB**), from **2010-10-16** to **2026-09-25**, scraped from [https://courtofappeal.lk](https://courtofappeal.lk)

- In **JSON**, **PDF** (100%), **TXT** (100%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (100%)

- In **English**

- 🎓 Cite as **[arXiv:2510.04124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04124) [cs.CL]**

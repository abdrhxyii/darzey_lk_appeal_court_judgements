Petitioners jointly made the application seeking writs of Mandamus

extend of 22 Acres belongs to J. L. D. Peiris Company. The

caretaker was K. Teeman Jayasekera. The said Company gifted 4

19.

As per the above averments, The Petitioners have failed to take any

steps to proof that they are the heirs of the K A. Lusihamy, despite

the Respondents demanded it. The above failure goes to the root of

this case, namely, the Petitioners’ entitlement, and The Court

accept that The Petitioners have failed in their tasks of claiming the

Land reserved for K A. Lusihamy. As such, the other materials are

not been considered.

20.

FOR THE REASONS ADUMBRATED ABOVE, THIS COURT IS

COMPELLED TO REJECT THE PETITIONERS’ APPLICATION

AND ACCORDINGLY THE WRIT APPLICATION IS DISMISSED

BUT WITHOUT AN ORDER FOR COST.

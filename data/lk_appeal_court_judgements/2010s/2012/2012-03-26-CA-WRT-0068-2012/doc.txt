S. SRISKANDARAJAH, J. (PICA)

Heard Counsel in support of this application.

The minutes of the Governing Council meeting 388 of the University

dated 11.08.2011, has considered the direction of this Court and had interviewed the

petitioner for the post of a Senior Lecturer of the Department of Business

Administration and as the recommendation submitted to the Governing Council was

not adequate, they have called for further report and after considering the report in

the meeting of 391 held on 14.10.2011 the Governing Council has decided that as the

Governing Council is not satisfied with his service and as it is not up to the required

standard, the Governing Council has decided not to appoint him. The petitioner in this

application is seeking a writ of certiorari to quash that decision. As the appointment is

vested in the Governing Council and the decision made by the Governing Council, in

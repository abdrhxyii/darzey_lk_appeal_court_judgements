The Appellant has not paid the brief fees. The Registrar of this

court was directed to issue notice on the Appellant under Rule 13(b)

of the Supreme Court (Court of Appeal Appellate Procedure Copies of

Records) Rules 1978, directing the appellant to pay the brief fees on or

before 03.02.2012. Accordingly a notice under said Rules has been

sent to the said appellant directing him to pay the brief fees on or

before the said date. The said notice which has been sent to the said

appellant has been returned by the postal authority with an

endorsement that the appellant has left the given address. I have

noted that the said appellant has not notified, the Registrar of this

court of any change of his address. In the said circumstances I am of

the view that the appellant has failed to comply with the said direction

given by this court. Hence I acting under Rule 13(b) of the said Rules

reject the appeal of the appellant without costs.

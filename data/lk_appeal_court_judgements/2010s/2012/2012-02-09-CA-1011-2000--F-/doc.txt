09.02.2012. ******

The respondent of this appeal has died. The appellant has to take

steps for substitution. Several notices which had been sent to the said

appellant have been returned by the postal authority with an

endorsement that the appellant cannot be found at the given address.

The appellant has not notified the Registrar of this Court of any change

of his address. In the said circumstances, I am of the view that the

appellant has not shown due diligence to prosecute this appeal. Hence I

acting under Rule 32 of the Supreme Court Rules dismiss the appeal of

the appellant without costs. Registrar of this court is directed to send

the main record to the relevant District Court with the order made by

this court.

Appeal dismissed.

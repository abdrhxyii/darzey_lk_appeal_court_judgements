SISIRA DE ABREW J.

Heard learned President Counsel for the Petitioner and the learned

Counsel for the 1st Respondent. The Petitioner S.M.M. Najeem in this

case has made an application in the High Court to intervene in Revision

Application No. 95/2010 filed in the High Court of Badulla. The learned

High Court Judge had dismissed this application for intervention on the

basis of the said S.M.M. Najeem was not a party in the case filed in the

Primary Court Case No. 88043. His application to the learned High

Court Judge was to intervene in the revision application. We therefore,

note that the reasons given by the learned High Court Judge in his order

dated 13.12.2011 are unacceptable. I

We therefore, set aside the order of the learned High Court Judge

dated 13.12.2011. The Petitioner S.M.M. Najeem is at liberty to re-agitate

his application before the High Court based on X15 filed in the High

Court. The learned High Court Judge is directed to consider the

application for intervention filed by S.M.M Najeem, produced as X15 in

the High Court. The respondents are entitled to file objections to the said

application for intervention X15 in the High Court. In view of this order

the learned President's Counsel for the Petitioner moves to withdraw the

Petition filed in this court. Accordingly, proceedings are terminated.

Petitioner is entitled to obtain a certified copy of this order on

payment of usual charges.

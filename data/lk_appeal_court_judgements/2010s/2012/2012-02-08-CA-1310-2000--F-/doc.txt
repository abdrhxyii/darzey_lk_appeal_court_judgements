Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

Appellant has not paid the brief fees. Hence the Registrar of this Court

was directed to issue notice on the Appellant under Rule 13(b) of the

Supreme Court (Court of Appeal-Appellate Procedure Copies of Records)

Rule 1978, directing the Appellant to pay the brief fees on or before

06.02.2012. Accordingly on notice under said Rule has been sent to the said

Appellant directing him to pay the brief fees on or before the said date. Said

notice has been returned by the postal Authority with an endorsement that

there is no such person at the given address.

In the said circumstance I am of the view that the Appellant has failed

to comply with the said direction given by this Court. Hence I acting under

said Rules reject the appeal of the Appellant without costs. Registrar of this

Court is directed to send the main record to the relevant District Court with

the order made by this Court.

Appeal rejected.

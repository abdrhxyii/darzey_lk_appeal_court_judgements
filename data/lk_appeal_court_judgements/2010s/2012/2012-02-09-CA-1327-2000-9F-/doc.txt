The Appellant has not paid the brief fees. Registrar of this

court was directed to issue notice on the appellant under Rule 13(b)

of the Supreme Court (Court of Appeal Appellate Procedure Copies of

Records) Rules 1978 directing the appellant to pay the brief fees on

or before 08.02.2012. Accordingly a notice under said Rules has

been sent to the said appellant directing him to pay the brief fees on

or before the said date. The appellant has failed to comply with the

said direction given by this court. Hence I acting under Rule 13(b) of

the said Rules reject the appeal of the said appellant without costs.

The Registrar of this court is directed to send the main record

to the relevant District Court with the order made by this court.

Appeal rejected.

C.A. 1382/00 (F) D.C. Matugama Case No: 1780/L.

BEFORE : Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

COUNSEL : Defendant-Appellant is absent and unrepresented.

Niluka Dissanayake for the Plaintiff-Respondent.

DECIDED ON : 16.02.2012

********

Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

Appellant has not paid the brief fees. Hence the Registrar of this Court

was directed to issue notice under Rule 13(b) of the Supreme Court (Court

of Appeal-Appellate Procedure Copies of Records) Rule 1978, directing the

Appellant to pay the brief fees on or before 15.02.2012. Accordingly on

notice under said Rule has been sent to the said Appellant directing him to

pay the brief fees on or before the said date.

The Appellant has failed to comply with the said direction given by

this Court. Hence I acting under said Rules reject the appeal of the Appellant

without costs.

Registrar of this Court is directed to send the main record to the

relevant District Court with the order made by this Court.

Appeal rejected.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

Respondent-respondent -

Respondents. Complainant Petitioner-Respondents in this case filed an

application in the Magistrate's Court in terms of section 98 of the Criminal

Procedure Code seeking an order against the Petitioner in this case when the

Petitioners were taking step to draw up 33000 V electricity line through

1

Mihindu Mawatha, in Tangalle. The Complainant Petitioner-Respondent live

at Mihindu Mawatha, Tangalle. Learned Magistrate by his order dated

04.05.2011 issued an interim order restraining the Petitioner and

Respondent-Respondent-Respondents (CEB) from drawing up electricity

line over Mihindu Mawatha, Tangalle. Learned Magistrate thereafter after

hearing evidence of the parties by his order dated 24.08.2011 vacated the

order issued by learned Magistrate on 04.05.2011. As a result of the order

dated 24.08.2011 of the Magistrate the Petitioners and the Electricity Board

were at liberty to draw up electricity line over Mihindu Mawatha, Tangalle.

Being aggrieved by the said order of the learned Magistrate dated

24.08.2011, the Complainant Petitioners-Respondents filed a revision

application in the High Court of Tangalle moving to set-aside the said order

of the learned Magistrate.

Learned High Court Judge on 23.11.2011 issued an interim order

staying the operation of the learned Magistrate's order dated 24.08.2011.

Being aggrieved by the said order of the learned High Court Judge the

Petitioners in this case have filed the present petition seeking to set-aside the

order of the learned High Court Judge.

We note that the order issued by the learned High Court Judge on

23.11.2011 is only an interim order. The Petitioners by this petition seek an

interim order staying the operation of the learned High Court Judge's order

dated 23.11.20 11. We note that if this relief is granted there will be no case

for the High Court Judge to examine which has been filed before him. We

further note that if the interim relief is issued it will amount to final relief

being granted by this Court to the Petitioners. Complainant Petitioner-

2

..

Respondents have filed documents in Court ( in the Magistrate' s Court) that

if 33000 V electricity wires are drawn up as planed by the Petitioners and

the Electricity Board, it may cause various health problem to the residence

of the area.

Learned State Counsel appearing for the Electricity Board submitted

that the petition by the Petitioners is pre mature. When we consider all these

matters we are of the opinion that we must permit the learned High Court

Judge to inquire into the complaint made by the Complainant Petitioners-

Respondents. When we consider all these matters we hold the view that

there is no merit in the petition filed by the Petitioner. Considering all these

matters we refuse to grant an interim order prayed for by the Petitioners and

also refuse to issue notice on the Respondents. We dismiss the petition.

Petition dismissed.

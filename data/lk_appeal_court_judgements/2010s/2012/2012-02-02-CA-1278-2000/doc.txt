Before Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

Counsel

unrepresented.

Decided on :

02.02.2012

Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

Appellant and the Respondent are absent and unrepresented.

Appellant has not paid the brief fees. Hence the Registrar of this court was

directed to issue notice on the Appellant under Rule 13(b) of the Supreme

Court (Court of Appeal Appellate Procedure Copies of Records) Rules 1978

directing the Appellant to pay the brief fees on or before 01/02/2012.

Accordingly a notice under said Rules has been sent to the said Appellant

directing him to pay the brief fees on or before the said date. The said notice

has been returned by the postal authority with an endorsement that the

Appellant has left the given address. I have noted that the Appellant has not

notified, the Registrar of this court of any change of his address. In the said

circumstances I am of the view that the Appellant has failed to comply with

the said direction given by this court. Hence I acting under Rule 13(b) of the

said Rules reject the Appeal of the said Appellant without costs.

The Registrar is directed to send the main case record to the relevant

District Court with the order made by this court. ,--

Appeal rejected.

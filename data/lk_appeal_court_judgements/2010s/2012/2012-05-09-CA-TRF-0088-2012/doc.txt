In this matter Counsel for the Petitioner is seeking to

have this case transferred to the District Court of Maho which is the

closest Court to Nikaweratiya. The Counsel, also draws my attention

to 'P2' wherein the District Judge of Nikaweratiya made observations

that both parties consent to have this case transferred and that both

parties have not expressed any objections since the court house at

Maho is the closest to the Nikaweratiya District Court. There cannot be

any inconvenience to the parties and the parties have not expressed so

III the District Court. Accordingly I make order transferring this case

to the District Court of Maho and the District Judge of Nikaweratiya

IS directed to send the main case record to the District Court of

Maho as soon as practicable.

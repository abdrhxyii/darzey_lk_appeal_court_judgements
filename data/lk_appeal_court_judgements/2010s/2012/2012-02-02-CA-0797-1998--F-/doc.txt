CA 797 198(F) DC(Maho )28321M

Before Anil Gooneratne, J

Counsel Appellant and Respondent absent and unrepresented.

Decided on 02.02.2012

Anil Gooneratne, J

This matter came up before this court on 17.1l.2011 both parties were absent and

unrepresented. Registrar's minute indicates that the brief fees have not been deposited by

the appellant. Accordingly this court directed the Registrar to issue 13 (b) notice on the

appellant as well as the registered attorney. The said notice has been dispatched by the

Registrar of this Court on 23 .11.20 11 to the appellant and his registered attorney as well

as the respondents. Notice dispatch in the appellant has been returned with the

endorsement. There is no such person resident in the given address. This sent to the

respondent there is no endorsement has provide the notice has been returned. However,

notices dispatched to the respective registered attorneys has not been returned. Perusal of

the docket court cannot find any information with regard to any change of address. In

these circumstances, it appears to this court have failed to exercise due diligence to

prosecute this appeal. This court acting in terms of Rule 13(b) and 34 of the Supreme

Court Rules has no alternative, but reject this appeal. Appeal dismissed. No costs.

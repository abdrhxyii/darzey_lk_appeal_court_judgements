D.C Pan dura Case No: 252/RE

Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

Appellant and the Respondent are absent

and unrepresented.

09.02.2012. ******

The Appellant has not paid the brief fees. The Registrar of this

court was directed to issue notice on the appellant under Rule 13(b)

of the Supreme Court (Court of Appeal Appellate Procedure Copies of

Records) Rules 1978 directing the appellant to pay the brief fees on or

before 08.02.2012. Accordingly a notice under said Rules has been

sent to the said appellant directing him to pay the brief fees on or

before the said date. The said notice has been returned by the postal

authority with an endorsement that there was no such person at the

given address. I have noticed that the appellant has not notified, the

Registrar of this court of any change of his address. In the said

circumstances I am of the view that the appellant has failed to comply

with the said direction given by this court. Hence I acting under Rule

13(b) of the said Rules reject the appeal of the said appellant without

costs.

2

The Registrar of this court is directed to send the main case

record to the relevant District Court with the order made by this court.

Appeal rejected.

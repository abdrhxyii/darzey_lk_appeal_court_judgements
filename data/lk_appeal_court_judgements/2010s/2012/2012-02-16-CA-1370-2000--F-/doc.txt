C.A. 1370/00 (F) D.C. Matugama Case No: 1951P.

BEFORE : Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

COUNSEL : Appellant and the Respondent are absent and

unrepresented.

DECIDED ON : 16.02.2012

********

Upaly Abeyrathne, J.

Appellant has not paid the brief fees. Hence the Registrar of this Court

was directed to issue notice under Rule 13(b) of the Supreme Court (Court of

Appeal-Appellate Procedure Copies of Records) Rule 1978, directing the

Appellant to pay the brief fees on or before 15.02.2012. Accordingly on notice

under said Rule has been sent to the said Appellant directing him to pay the

brief fees on or before the said date. Said notice has been returned by the

postal Authority without any endorsement.

In the said circumstance I am of the view that the Appellant has failed

to comply with the said direction given by this Court. Hence I acting under

said Rules reject the appeal of the Appellant without costs. Registrar of this

Court is directed to send the main record to the relevant District Court with

the order made by this Court.

Appeal rejected.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

Heard counsel in support of this application. The

petitioner in this application is seeking a writ of

certiorari to quash the decision of the council of the 1st

respondent dated 14.12.2000 marked P11A to exhonarate the

25 th respondent from the charges leveled against him by

the charge sheet dated 04.06.1998. The petitioner has also

sought a writ of mandamus directing the 1st respondent

University to initiate the disciplinary inquiry against

the 25 th respondent in terms of the Establishment code of

the Uni versi ty. The petitioner is not attached to the

said University at present and as such the petitioner has

not shown by the said decision of the University Council

dated 14.12.2000 that his rights are affected and further

the said decision was made in 2000 and the petitioner is

seeking to quash the said decision in this application in

•

2012 and for these reasons, this court refuses to issue

notice on the respondents.

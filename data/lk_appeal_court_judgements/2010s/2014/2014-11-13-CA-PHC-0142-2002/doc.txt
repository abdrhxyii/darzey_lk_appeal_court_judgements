Learned Counsel for the Respondent-Respondent-Appellant

submits that this Court has no jurisdiction to entertain this appeal

since the appellate power to challenge the decisions of the High Court

made in terms of the provisions contained in the Maintenance Act No.

37 of 1999 is vested with the Supreme Court. The law referred to

above is conceded by the learned Counsel for the respondent as well.

In terms of Section 14(2) of the Maintenance Act, the appeals

filed to challenge the decision of a High Court Judge is to be preferred

..

to the Supreme Court. Therefore, having looked at Section 14 (2) of

the Maintenance Act No. 37 of 1999, we decide that this Court has no

jurisdiction to entertain this appeal. Therefore, this appeal is

dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

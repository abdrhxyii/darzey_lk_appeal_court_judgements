K.T. Chitrasiri,J.

Both Counsel submit that the parties have settled the

dispute in this case on the following terms:

1) Plaintiff-Respondent agrees to sell Lot 2 In plan

2014/56 drawn by S Kumari Wijekoon licensed

surveyor; to the defendant-appellants or to a person

nominated by them.

2) For the purpose of purchase of the land referred to

above; the Defendant-Appellants are prepared to pay

Rs: 743,000/- to the Plaintiff-Respondent as the sale

pnce.

1

3) The sale to be taken place, according to the terms

referred to above, on or before 31.03.2015.

4) If the Defendant-Appellants fail to comply with the said

terms of settlement, appeal is to stand dismissed and

the plaintiff-respondent is entitled to obtain writ

accordingly, without notice to the Defendant-Appellants.

5) If the Plaintiff-Respondent fails to execute the deed, then

the Defendant-Appellants are entitled to have it executed

by an application made to the Registrar of District Court

of Kandy, upon depositing the aforesaid sum of Rs:

743,000/-. In such an event the deed is to be executed

within three months from 31.03.2015 having deposited

Rs: 743,000/-. If such an occasion arises; the Registrar

of the District Court is directed to execute a deed having

him satisfied as to the deposit of Rs: 743,000/- in favour

of the plaintiff-respondent.

The plan bearing No: 2014/56 IS to be filed into the

docket. Accordingly, the judgment dated 20.08.1998 is set

aside.

Learned District Judge of Kandy is directed to enter

decree in accordance with the terms referred to above.

2

Subject to the above variations, this appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

Judge of the Court of Appeal

Malinie Gunaratne,J.

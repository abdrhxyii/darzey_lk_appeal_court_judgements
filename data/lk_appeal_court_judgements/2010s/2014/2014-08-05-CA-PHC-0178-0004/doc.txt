Learned State Counsel submits that this is an appeal where the High

Court Judge has exercised appellate power. In view of the decision in

Wickremasekera Vs. o.I.e. Police Station Ampara, [(2004) (1) SLR 257]

this Court does not have jurisdiction to hear such appeals. Accordingly,

this appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

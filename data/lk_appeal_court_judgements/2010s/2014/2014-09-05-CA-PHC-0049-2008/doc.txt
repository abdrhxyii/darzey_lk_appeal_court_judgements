When this matter was mentioned on 10.09.2013, it has

been recorded that the notice sent to the 1st appellant had been

returned with the endorsement that he IS dead. Thereafter, on an

application by the Counsel who appeared for the appellants, another

notice also had been sent to the heirs of the appellant. The said

letter also had been returned stating that he is dead. Today neither

loth of September 2013, three days had been given to take steps for

substitution. Not a single party is present before Court today. Accordingly,

we make order abating the appeal. Appeal abated.

Appeal abated.

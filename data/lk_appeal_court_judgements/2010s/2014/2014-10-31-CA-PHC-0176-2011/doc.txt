This is an appeal seeking to set aside the orders dated 20.09.2011

and 02.09.2009 of the learned High Court Judge and of the learned

Magistrate respectively. The 18t respondent namely, the Divisional Secretary

made an application on 25.03.2009 under the State Land (Recovery of

Possession) Act No: 7 of 1979 as amended, to recover possession of the land

referred to in the said application dated 25.03.2009. Having held an inquiry

on the aforesaid application, learned Magistrate made order on 09.09.2002

to eject the appellant from the land in question. Basically, the reason for the

aforesaid decision was that the appellant had failed to show cause, why he

should not be evicted from the land in question. Being aggrieved by the

aforesaid decision, the appellant filed a revision application in the High

Court.

When it was taken up before the learned High Court Judge once

again the appellant did not present any ground in order to succeed in his

revision application filed in the High Court. In this instance too, nothing is

submitted to show that the appellant is having any permit or written

authority issued by the State to be in occupation of the land in dispute.

In the cases of Muhandiram Vs. Chairman JEDB (1992 (1) SLR 110)

and Nirmal Paper Converters ( PVT ) Ltd. Vs. Sri Lanka Ports Authority

(1993 (1) SLR 219), it was held that unless a valid permit or written

authority is produced by the person who claims to be in possession of

State Land, then he is not entitled to be in occupation of that land. Relying

upon the aforesaid decisions, we decide that this appeal has no merit.

Accordingly, this appal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

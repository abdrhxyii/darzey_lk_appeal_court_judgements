The Registrar of this Court had issued notices to all the parties

directing them to be present in this Court today. They are not present

in Court. Those notices have been sent under registered cover. Notices

had been sent to the addresses given in the petition of appeal. The

appellant has not even paid the brief fees. Accordingly, acting upon

Rule 13(b) of the Supreme Court Rules, this appeal is rejected.

Appeal rejected.

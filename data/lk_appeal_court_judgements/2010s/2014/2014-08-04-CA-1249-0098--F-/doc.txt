This matter is being mentioned today to effect the

substitution in the room of the deceased 9th Defendant-

Respondent. Counsel for the appellant was given several dates to

effect the aforesaid substitution. Up to now no substitution papers

appeal diligently. Unless the substitution is effected in the room of

the deceased 9th Defendant-Respondent this appeal cannot be

taken for argument. In the circumstances Court makes an order

abating the appeal.

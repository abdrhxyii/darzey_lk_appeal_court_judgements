This is an appeal to reVIse the orders dated 20.10.2010 and

30.08.2012 made by the learned Magistrate and the learned High Court

Judge, respectively. Learned High Court Judge in his order dated

30.08.2012 has dismissed the revision application filed before him on

the basis that the same had been filed without exercising the right to

file an appeal that the petitioner could have invoked as of a right.

At this stage, learned Senior State Counsel brings to the notice of

Court that the law does not provide for an appeal to be filed when an

order is made confiscating a vehicle upon a conviction made consequent

to an offence been committed under the Animals Act. Accordingly, she

moves that this matter be sent back for re-hearing enabling the learned

Having considered the aforesaid submissions, we find that the

learned High Court Judge has misdirected himself when he was of the

opinion that the appellant had the right to file an appeal to revise the

order of the learned Magistrate. Accordingly, we set aside the order

dated 30.08.2012 of the learned High Court Judge of Badulla.

Learned High Court Judge is directed to consider the merits of the

revision application made by the petitioner-appellant and thereafter to

make an order according to law.

The Registrar is directed to send the original record with this

Order to the High Court of Badulla for the learned High Court Judge to

take further steps as directed.

Appeal allowed.

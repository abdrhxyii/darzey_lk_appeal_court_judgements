K.T.Chitrasiri, J.

Learned Counsel for the Respondent submits that this is an

appeal filed to challenge a decision made in the High Court in

respect of an arbitral award being enforcement. When such a decision

is made by the High Court in Colombo, the aggrieved party should

obtain leave from the Supreme Court and then proceed with the

appeal. This procedure is laid down in section 37 (2) of the Arbitration

Act No. 11 of 1995.

The Appellant has failed to follow the procedure referred to in

the said Act No. 11 of 1995. Learned Counsel for the Appellant

concedes this position. In view of the above provisions of the law,

2

this Court has no jurisdiction to entertain this appeal. Accordingly,

this appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

K.T. Chitrasiri,J.

Learned Counsel for the Petitioner submitted that the Petitioner in this case

has failed to file an appeal to the Supreme Court due to the procedural law

found in Section 88 of the Civil Procedure Code. It is the reason for the

Petitioner to have filed this application seeking to exercise revisionary

jurisdiction of this Court and to grant relief as prayed for in the Petition.

However, having considered the decisions, in Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka Vs.

Wijewardena and four others (2010) BLR 233, Austra lanka Exporters (Pvt.)

Ltd. Vs. Indian Bank and also the Judgment in Ana Weeratunga Vs. Sepala

Ekanayake, C.A. (P.H.C.) 204/2006 we are of the opinion that this Court has

no jurisdiction to entertain revision applications in which the decisions made

~ i I I ! f I

by the High Court of the Western Province (exercising civil jurisdiction) are

being challenged.

In view of the decisions referred to above, this application stands dismissed.

Application dismissed.

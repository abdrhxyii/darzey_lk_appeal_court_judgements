This is an appeal seeking to set aside the judgment dated 04.11.2009 of the

learned High Court Judge of Ratnapura wherein he has held that the High

Court has no jurisdiction on the basis that the grants issued under Section

19(4) of the Land Development Ordinance do not fall within the devolved

subjects as per the Constitution.

The grant subjected to in this case had been given by His Excellency the

President of the Republic of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, it is clear that the

matter in issue does not come within the Provincial Council list referred to

in the 13th amendment to the Constitution. Therefore, we do not see any

error when the learned High Court Judge dismissed this writ application

filed by the appellant due to lack of jurisdiction.

Accordingly, this appeal is dismissed.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

W.M.M. MALINIE GUNARATNE, J.

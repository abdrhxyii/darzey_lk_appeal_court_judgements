This is a Revision Application to revise the order confiscating the

lorry made pursuant to a conviction by the Magistrate for an offence under

the Forest Ordinance. However only the petition and an affidavit had

been filed in this application without filing any other documents relating

to this case.

Therefore, counsel for the petitioner moves to withdraw this

application reserving his right to file fresh papers with the necessary

documents. Application for withdrawal is allowed. Accordingly

application is dismissed.

Application dismissed.

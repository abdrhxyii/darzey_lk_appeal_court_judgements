K.T. Chitrasiri,J.

Learned Counsel for the respondent moves that this appeal be dismissed

since the Petition of Appeal had been tendered in violation of Rule 2(I)(a) of

the Court of Appeal (Procedure for Appeals from High Courts) Rules 1988.

Accordingly, he submitted that the Petition of Appeal in this case had been

filed beyond the 14 day period referred to in the aforesaid Rule 2(I)(a).

Relying upon the aforesaid Rules learned Counsel for the respondent moves

that this appeal be dismissed for the reason that the petition of appeal had

not been filed within 14 days from the impugned decision.

1

I

The impugned decision in this case had been made on 14.11.2006

by the learned High Court Judge in Polonnaruwa. Then the Petition of Appeal

should have been filed within 14 days from 14.11.2006 as required by Rule

2(1)(a) of the Court of Appeal Rules 1988. In this instance, the Petition of

Appeal had been tendered on 05.12.1006 even though the date of the Petition

is typed as 01 st December 2006 in the petition. The date on which the petition

of Appeal was tendered is evident by looking at the seal found on the first

page of the petition of Appeal. The aforesaid Petition of Appeal had been filed

with a motion which is found at page 213 in the Original Record. In that

motion, the date-stamp of the Polonnaruwa High Court is also found and the

date thereon is clearly seen as 05.12.2006. Furthermore, the Registrar of the

High Court of Polonnaruwa by his handwriting has written that the Petition

was tendered at 9.50 a.m. on the 5th December 2006. Learned Counsel for

the appellant submits that he cannot dispute those facts. Therefore, it is

clear that the Petition of Appeal had been tendered only on 05.12.2006.

If the Petition of Appeal had been tendered on 05.12.2006, then

the Petition of Appeal had been filed beyond 14 days, referred to in Rule 2(1)(a)

of the Court of Appeal Rules 1988.

Having considered the above circumstances, we decide that the

Petition of Appeal lodged in this case has been filed in contravention of the

Rule 2(1)(a) of the Court of Appeal (Procedure for Appeals from High Courts)

Rules 1988.

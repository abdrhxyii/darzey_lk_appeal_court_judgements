Appellant is present in court. This is an appeal filed to

challenge the decision dated 24.10.2002 wherein the learned

High Court Judge has exercised appellate powers.

Section 14 (2) of the Maintenance Act No 37/1999 stipulates that any person dissatisfied with an order made by a High

Court in the exercise of its appellate jurisdiction may

prefer an appeal therefrom to the Supreme Court on a

question of law, with the leave of the High Court ...

In view of the above provision in law, this court has no jurisdiction to entertain this appeal.

Accordingly we make order dismissing this appeal.

Appeal dismissed.

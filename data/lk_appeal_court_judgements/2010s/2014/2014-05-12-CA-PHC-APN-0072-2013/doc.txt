A.W.A. Salam, J

The suspect in respect of whom the application for bail has been made in

the High Court of Galle had been in incarceration for one year and eight

months. The minimum sentence mandated for this offence with which

the suspect is charged is one year and maximum is fIxed as 5 years. The

Learned Counsel for the petitioner cited the judgement In

CA(PHC)APN38/2009. Even though the judgement cited is not similar on

all fours, yet it is relevant to the present issue.

Quite apart from the above the suspect in this case had been on remand

for one year and eight months without his being charged and there is no

assurance how long more will take for the suspect to be charged in the

appropriate court. The unusual length of time during which he has been

on remand awaiting the charge sheet by itself constitutes exceptional

circumstances.

I I

2

As such we direct that the suspect be released on bail upon furnishing

cash bail in a sum of rupees one hundred thousand (Rs.100,000/-) and

surety bail in a sum of rupees one million (Rs.100,000/-) with four

sureties from and among the family members including the spouse, if

living. In addition the passport of the suspect to be impounded and he is

required to report to the Magistrate's Court of Galle on the last working

day of every month between 10.30 a.m and 2.00 p.m.

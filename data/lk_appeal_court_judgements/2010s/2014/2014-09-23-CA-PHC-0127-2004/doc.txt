This is an appeal to set aside the decision dated 22.04.2004, made by the

Learned High Court Judge of Rathnapura wherein he has exercised appellate

powers. In terms of the decision in Wickramasekara Vs. OIC Ampara (2004

(1)SLR257), this court has no jurisdiction to entertain such an appeal.

Accordingly, this appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

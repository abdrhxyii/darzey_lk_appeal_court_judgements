Learned State Counsel brings to the notice of court that this is an appeal to

challenge a decision where the learned High Court Judge has exercised

appellate powers. Accordingly, she submits that this court has no jurisdiction

to entertain this appeal.

Being aggrieved by the decision of the learned Magistrate the appellant has

filed an appeal dated 02.01.2004 in the High Court of Chilaw. (petition of

appeal is found at page 71 in the appeal brief) The learned High Court in

Chilaw by her order dated 09.06.2005 (vide at page 17 in the appeal brief) has

dismissed the said appeal. The appellant having failed to obtain relief from the

High Court has filed this appeal on 17.06.2005. Therefore, it is clear that the

learned High Court Judge has exercised appellate powers in this instance. In

Mr. Amila Palliyage submits that this is an application to claim a

vehicle by its absolute owner who is the appellant in this appeal,

pursuant to a confiscation order made by the Magistrate in an action

filed under the Animals Act. He further informs Court that in view of

the decision in Orient Financial Services Corporation Limited Vs.

Range Forest Officer, Ampara, (SC Appeal 120/2011) absolute owner

cannot claim a vehicle confiscated by a Magistrate upon conviction for

an offence committed under the Law.

In view of the aforesaid decision in the Supreme Court, the appellant

being the absolute owner, he cannot claim the vehicle subjected to in

this case. Accordingly, this appeal is dismissed.

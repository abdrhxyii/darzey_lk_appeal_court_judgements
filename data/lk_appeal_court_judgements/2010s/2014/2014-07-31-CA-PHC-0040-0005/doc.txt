It is brought to the notice of Court that this is an appeal filed to

challenge the decision of the learned High Court Judge in Kegalle where

he has exercised appellate powers. In terms of the decision of

Wickremasaker Vs. Ole. Ampara, {2004 (1) SLR 257} this Court has

no jurisdiction to entertain such appeals. Accordingly, this appeal is

dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

W.M.M.Malinie Gunaratne,J.

I agree.

Learned Deputy Solicitor General submits to Court having

perused the record that there is no formal defence called as per

Section 200 (1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act. This Court

Code. In the above circumstances, we have no alternative but to

set aside the conviction and sentence and send the case back for

fresh trial.

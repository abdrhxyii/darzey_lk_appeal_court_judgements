24.07.2014

Counsel for the Appellants and the Counsel for the 1 st Respondent

submit that this dispute is now settled among the parties on the following

terms:-

1. The petitioner- appellants are to pay Rs.800,000/- to the

1 st Respondent within a period of two months from today.

Accordingly, the appellants are to pay Rs 800,0001- on or before

24/9/2014 to the 1 st respondent Co-operative Society.

2. In the event the appellants pay Rs.800,0001- as mentioned before,

the 1 st respondent undertakes to cancel the Mortgage Bond bearing

No.29 dated 04/311998 attested by E.K.Nanayakkara Notary Public

within a period of one week, from the date of payment, made by the

appellants.

3. If the appellants fail to pay the money referred to above as

undertaken, the 1 st respondent Co-operative Society has the right

to enforce the order dated 24.11.2005 delivered by the 3rd

respondent who is the Arbitrator.

4. The appellants are to make the payment at the office of the

1 st respondent Co-operative Society.

5. The appellants are entitled to have the Mortgage Bond cancelled

by making an application to the Registrar of the High Court of

Kegalle, in the event the 1 st respondent fails to cancel the

Mortgage Bond upon the payment of Rs.800,0001- made by

the appellants.

The parties who are present in Court sign the record accepting the

aforesaid terms of settlement.

Accordingly, this Court makes an order directing the parties to abide

by the terms of settlement mentioned above. In VIew of the above

settlement the proceedings in this Court are terminated.

Proceedings terminated.

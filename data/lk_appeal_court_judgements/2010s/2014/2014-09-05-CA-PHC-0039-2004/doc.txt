******** K. T. Chitrasiri, J.

The letter dated 24.07.2014 sent by the Registrar of this

Court to the appellant under registered cover had been returned with the

endorsement that there is no such person at the given address. This

notice had been sent to the address given in the petition of appeal.

However, learned State Counsel informs Court that this IS an appeal

filed to challenge a decision made by the Provincial High Court Judge in

Anuradhapura where he has exercised appellate power. In terms of the

decision in Wickremasekera V s. O.I.C. Police Station Ampara (2004 (1)

SLR 257) this Court has no jurisdiction to entertain such appeals.

Accordingly, this appeal IS dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

07.08.2014

Learned State Counsel submits that the learned High Court Judge,

who made the decision that is being challenged in this revision

application, has not addressed his mind to the main ground of appeal

upon which the revision application was filed in the High Court. It is

made clear by the paragraph found in page 3 of the decision marked

PI0 of the learned High Court Judge. In that decision learned High

Court Judge has clearly stated that he is not expressing any opinion

as to manner in which the learned Magistrate has come to the decision

that was challenged before him. Accordingly, learned State Counsel

moves that this matter be sent back to the learned High Court Judge

to hear and consider the merits of the revision application dated

10.03.2010. Learned Counsel for the petitioner has no objection to

this application.

We note that the learned High Court Judge has not addressed his

mind to the issue that had been raised in the petition dated

10.03.2010. Therefore, we do not see any error III the application

1

made by the learned State Counsel. Accordingly, the judgment dated

27.06.2013 of the learned High Court Judge of Kurunegala is set

aside. Learned High Court Judge of Kurunegala is directed to

commence the proceedings afresh in respect of the application dated

10.03.2010.

Accordingly, the proceedings in this appeal are terminated.

Proceedings terminated.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

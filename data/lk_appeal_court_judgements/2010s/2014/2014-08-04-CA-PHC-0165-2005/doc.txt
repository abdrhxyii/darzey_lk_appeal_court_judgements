K. T. Chitrasiri, J.

This is an appeal filed against the decision that had been

delivered by the learned High Court Judge of Anuradhapura

where she has exercised appellate powers. In terms of

Wickremasekera Vs. D.I.C Ampara, (2004 (1) SLR 257) this Court

does not have the appellate jurisdiction when challenging a

decision in which the High Court Judge has exercised appellate

powers. Accordingly this Court has no jurisdiction to entertain

this appeal. Appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

This is an application for relisting. This Court made order

on 31.01.2014 abating the appeal. In the petition dated 31.03.2014 that

was filed seeking to vacate the order of abatement dated 31.01.2014, it

is stated that the necessary papers are filed and annexed to the said

petition in order to substitute the heirs of the deceased 11th, 12th and

13th defendant-respondents.

The person sought to be substituted in the room of the 11th

and 13th defendant-respondents is a child of a brother of the deceased

11th and 13th defendant-respondents. In those affidavits nothing is

mentioned to show that those two defendants died issueless.

Furthermore, the sisters and brothers of the deceased 11th and 13th

defendant-respondents are also not been referred to in those two

2

applications. Therefore, the petitioner has failed to show that the person

sought to be substituted is the correct person who is entitled to proceed

with this appeal on behalf of the 11th and 13th defendant-respondents.

In VIew of the above, learned Counsel for the appellant

submits that he will take necessary steps to show the manner in which

the person sought to be substituted becomes the proper and correct

person to proceed with this appeal and accordingly, he undertakes to file

fresh papers. In view of the above circumstances, this application is

dismissed without costs.

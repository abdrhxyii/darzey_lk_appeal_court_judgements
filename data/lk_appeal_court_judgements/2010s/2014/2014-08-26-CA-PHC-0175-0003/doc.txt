K. T. CRITRASIRI, J.

Learned State Counsel raising a preliminary objection submits that

in view of the decisions in Wickremasekera Vs. OIC Police Station

Ampara, (2004 (1) SLR 257) this Court has no jurisdiction to

entertain this appeal. This is an appeal filed to challenge the

decision made on 14.11.2002 by the learned High Court Judge of

Negombo where he has exercised appellate powers. In view of the

decision in Wickremasekera Vs. OIC Police Station Ampara

(Supra) this Court has no jurisdiction to entertain appeals filed to

challenge the decisions of the Provisional High Court where the

said High Court has exercised appellate powers. Accordingly this

Court has no jurisdiction to entertain this appeal. Therefore this

appeal is dismissed. Learned Counsel for the appellant at this

stage moves to have this appeal converted to a revision application.

However this Court is not inclined to do so since it may lead to

negate the Supreme Court decision made in Wickramasekera Vs.

D.l.e Police Station Ampara in the event the decision in such an

application is made in favour of the appellant.

For the aforesaid reasons this appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

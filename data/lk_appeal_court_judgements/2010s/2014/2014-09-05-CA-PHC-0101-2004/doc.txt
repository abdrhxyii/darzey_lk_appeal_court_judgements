K. T. Chitrasiri, J.

The Registrar of this Court has sent a notice to the

appellant directing him to pay the brief fees. The said letter had been

sent to the address given in the petition of appeal under registered

cover. However, the appellant has not paid the brief fees. Therefore

acting under rule 13(b) of Supreme Court Rules this Court makes an order

rejecting the appeal. At this stage it is necessary to note that this an

appeal filed to challenge the decision where the High Court Judge has

exercised appellate powers. In terms of the decision in Wickremasekera

Vs. O.I.C. Police Station Ampara, (2004 (1) SLR 257) this Court has no

jurisdiction to entertain such an appeal. Appeal rejected.

Appeal rejected.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

W.M.M. Malinie Gunarathne, J.

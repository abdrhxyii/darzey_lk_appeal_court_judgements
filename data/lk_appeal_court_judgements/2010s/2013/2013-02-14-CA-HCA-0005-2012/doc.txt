Heard both Counsel in support of their respective cases.

1

This is a Habeas Corpus Application filed by the petitioner

moving this Court to direct the respondent to produce the corpus in

Court.

The petitioner in this case alleges that some people on

29.10.2011 around 11.30 a.m. came and arrested her husband from the

house of her mother-in-law. At the time of the arrest, those who arrested

her husband has claimed that they were from the Criminal Investigation

Department. The petitioner has, at this time, seen four persons in army

2

uniform had surrounded the house of the mother-in-law of the petitioner.

The respondents have however denied the arrest of the corpus.

Considering all these matters, we decide to direct the Magistrate to hold

an inquiry into this matter. We direct the Magistrate of Colombo to

hold an inquiry on this matter. Registrar of this Court is directed to send

a certified copy of the entire case record to the Magistrate's Court.

Petitioner is directed to move this Court by way of a motion after the

inquiry is concluded by the Magistrate.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

P.W.D.C. JAYATHILAKA, J.

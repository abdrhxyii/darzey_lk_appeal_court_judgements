It appears from the minutes that the appellant has been given several

dates to take steps for substitution. But the appellant is absent and

unrepresented. Hence it seems that the appellant or the heirs of the

appellant has failed to show due diligence in taking necessary steps for

the purpose of prosecuting this appeal. In the said circumstances, I

dismiss the appeal of the appellant without costs.

The Registrar is directed to send the main record to the relevant District

Court.

Appeal dismissed.

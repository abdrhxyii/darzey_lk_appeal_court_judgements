Ms. Purnima Gunasekera watching the interests of the heirs of the deceased 4A defendant-respondent

12th December, 2013

Counsel, Ms. Purnima Gunasekera watching the interests of

the heirs of the 4A defendant-respondent informs Court that the 4A

defendant-respondent has passed away. The person who is present in

Court on behalf of the deceased 4A defendant-respondent informs Court

that the plaintiff-appellant too is dead. No appearance is made on behalf

of the heirs of the deceased plaintiff-appellant.

Notices had been sent to the appellant and to the registered

Attorney of the appellant under registered cover by the Registrar of this

Court directing them to be present in this Court today. The notice sent to

the Registered Attorney of the appellant had been returned with the

endorsement that she has left the given address. Therefore, no further

steps can be taken by this Court. In the circumstances, Court makes

order abating the appeal.

Appeal is abated.

The accused is the suspect in respect of whom this revision

application has been filed against the refusal to enlarge him on

bail. He has been in incarceration for nearly two years.

The learned Senior State Counsel brings it to the notice of

Court that the accused is now facing another case where he is

indicted with double murder. Taking into consideration the

period of incarceration pending trial, we are of the opinion that

this a fit case to direct the accused to be released on bail. As such.

we set aside the impugned order of the learned High Court Judge

and direct the learned Magistrate of Marawila to enlarge the

suspect Lenard Luwis Washington alias Chandana alias Sumith

on the bail on the following conditions.

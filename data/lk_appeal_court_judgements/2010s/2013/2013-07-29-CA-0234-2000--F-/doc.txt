Learned counsel for the 5A defendant-respondent

brings to the notice of court that a decision had already been made

by this court in a revision application filed by the 4th defendant-

appellant herself challenging the same decision which is being

canvassed in this appeal. A certified copy of the said judgment is

being tendered to Court by the learned counsel for the respondent.

Upon perusal of the said judgment, it is seen that this court

has made a decision in the Case bearing No. C.A. 904/2000 where

an application had been made to canvass the judgment and the

interlocutory decree entered on 10.11.1999 in the case bearing No.

4300 I P filed in the District Court of Kurunegala.

1

\

The appeal at hand also has been filed to challenge the

same judgment dated 1 0 I 11 I 1999 made in the case bearing No.

4300IP filed in the District Court of Kurunegala. The judgment in

the Revision application (C.A. 90412000) which is being tendered

to court today is handed over to the learned counsel for the

appellant for perusal. He concedes that the application made in

the revision application is also to revise and set aside the

judgment and interlocutory decree entered in the case bearing No.

4300IP

In the circumstances, it is clear that this court has already

delivered a judgment in another application made to this court to

challenge the judgment which is being canvassed in this appeal.

Therefore this court has no jurisdiction to consider the same issue in

this appeal. Accordingly the appeal is dismissed without costs.

The Registrar is directed to file the document which contains

the judgment delivered in the case bearing No. C.A. 90412000,

into this docket.

Appeal dismissed.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL.

Having argued the appeal, both Counsel submit that the parties have

settled the dispute in the following manner.

(1) Parties agree to accept the V2 share gIven by the learned District

Judge to the 1 st Defendant and to have it remained as mentioned in

the Judgment dated 21.05.1998.

(2) The 2nd Defendant agree to give 8 perches from his V2 share to the

Plaintiff-Appellant restricting his share to the remaining portion of

the said V2 share allocated to him by the learned District Judge.

06th February, 2013

This is an application for Special Leave to Appeal against the order of

the Court of Appeal dated 2110512012 refusing an application made in

terms of Section 46 of the Judicature Act seeking the transfer of a case

pending in the District Court of Minuwangoda to an appropriate Court

in which fair and impartial trial could be conducted without any fear

on the part of any of the parties to the case.

Having heard learned Counsel for all the parties we are inclined to

formally grant Special Leave to Appeal on the questions set out m

paragraph 17 of the Petition dated 0210712012. Learned Counsel

have also brought to the notice of Court that this case is scheduled to

be called in the District Court of Minuwangoda on 15 I 02 I 20 13 m

view of an undertaking to maintain the status quo given by reason of a

direction issued by this Court on 1910912012.

In these circumstances, and in view of the fact that the parties are all

of the view that fair trial should take place without any fear, all learned

Counsel are now agreed that it is best in all the circumstances of this

case for this Court to make an order transferring the case now in the

District Court of Minuwangada to the District Court of Negombo.

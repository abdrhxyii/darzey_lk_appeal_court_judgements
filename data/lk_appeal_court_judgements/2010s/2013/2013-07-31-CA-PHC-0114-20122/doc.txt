A.W.A.SALAM, J.

Appellant absent and unrepresented. On 12.03.2012, the

Registrar has been directed to prepare the appeal briefs and then notify the

parties. Subsequently, the Registrar has put up a minute dated 21.06.2013

by which he has informed Court, that the appellant has failed to deposit

brief fees. As such, the direction to prepare the briefs on 12.03.2013

appears to have been entered by inadvertence. The appellant has been

notified by notice dated 28.02.2013 to deposit the brief fees on or before

11.03.2013. The notice despatched to be served on the appellant has not

been returned and therefore factually presumed to have been served.

Court observes that although the notice of appeal has been signed

by the appellant's registered attorney, the Petition of Appeal has been

filed by the appellant without revoking the proxy of the registered

attorney. At this stage Counsel appearing for the appellant moves to

withdraw this appeal with liberty to file a fresh application. Application

for withdrawal is allowed. Appeal dismissed without cost.

Registrar is directed to send this order with the main case record

to the District of Galle.

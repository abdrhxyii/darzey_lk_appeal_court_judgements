K.T.CHITRASIRI, J.

Counsel for the Appellant concedes that in view of the amendment

to the Partition Act No: 17 of 1997 ~ the appellant should have filed a

leave to appeal application to challenge the impugned order dated

09.12.1998. In this instance the appellant has chosen to file a final

appeal. Therefore, learned counsel for the appellant moves to withdraw

this appeal. Application for withdrawal is allowed. Accordingly the appeal

is dismissed without costs.

Appeal dismissed.

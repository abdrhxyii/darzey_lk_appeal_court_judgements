UPALY ABEYRATHNE, J.

Defendant-Appellant is absent and unrepresen ted

Ikram Mohamed P.C. for the Plaintiff-Respondent

09.09.2013.

It is apparent from the minutes of the docket that the

Defendant-Appellant has died. It also appears from the minutes

that the appellant was represented by his Attorney. The learned

Counsel for the Appellant has taken 9 days from 24.10.2011 in

order to file papers for substitution. It is apparent from the said

conduct, that the heirs of the appellant have failed to show due

diligence in taking necessary steps for the purpose of prosecuting

this appeal. In the said circumstances, I dismiss the appeal of the

appellant without costs.

Registrar is directed to send the record back to the

relevant District Court.

Appeal dismissed.

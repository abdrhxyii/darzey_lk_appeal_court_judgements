S. SRISK.ANDARAJAH, J. (P/CA)

1

Counsel for the Petitioner supports the application and

submits that his liquor licence was suspended and virtually cancelled

and he has not complained in relation to that cancellation. But he

complains to Court in relation to a licence issued to the 8th Respondent.

The relief sought is for a Writ of Certiorari to quash the decision to issue

liquor licence to the 8th Respondent. The Petitioner has not complained

that his rights are affected by issuing a licence to the 8th respondent and

the Petitioner is in fact not affected by the issuance of the licence to the

8th Respondent. The Petitioner has not come to this case as a public

interest litigant and he has not complained to this Court that the

issuance of licence is against the public interest. As the Petitioner's right

2

is not affected by the issuance of licence to the 8th Respondent this Court

is not inclined to issue notice on the respondents.

PRESIDENT OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

W. M. M. MALINIE GUNARATNE, J.

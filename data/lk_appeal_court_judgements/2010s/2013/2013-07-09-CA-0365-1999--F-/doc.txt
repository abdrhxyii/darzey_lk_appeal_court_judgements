H.N.J. Perera, J.

Defendant-Appellant is absent and unrepresented. The notice have

been served on the Defendant-Appellant by registered post. He is not

present in Court. Counsel for the Plaintiff-Respondent moves that this

appeal be dismissed and also submits that this appeal cannot be

maintained in this court as there is no right of appeal from the impugned

order made by the learned District Judge of Mt. Lavinia. It appears to

this court that the appellant has failed to exercise due diligence to

prosecute this appeal. Accordingly, this appeal is dismissed.

Registrar is directed to send a copy of this order with the original

case record to the District Court of Mr. Lavinia.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL.

05.06.2013

Learned Counsel for the Appellant submits that the order that is being challenged

in this appeal is in respect of an order made consequent to the application made in the

petition dated 10.10.1997 which was supported on the 13.10.1997. The said petition

dated 10.10.1997 is found at page 38 of the brief. In that petition, among the other reliefs

an application for an interim injunction also had been made. The impugned order is in

respect of the said interim injunction referred to in the petition dated 10.10.1997. In

view of the above circumstances, learned Counsel for the appellant moves to withdraw

this appeal reserving his right to pursue the other reliefs referred to in the aforesaid

petition dated 10.10.1997.

Counsel for the respondent has no objection to this application. Application for

withdrawal of the appeal is allowed. Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed without costs.

The appeal is dismissed.

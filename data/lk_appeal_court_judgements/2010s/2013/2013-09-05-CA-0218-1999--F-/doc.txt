H.N. J. PERERA, J.

In this case, Court had issued notice on the defendants-

appellants and their registered Attorneys to deposit the appeal brief fees.

Notice issued on the 1 st defendant-appellant had been returned with the

endorsement that he is dead and at the same time, notice issued on the

registered Attorney had been returned with the endorsement that the

registered Attorney too had passed away. Notice issued on the 2nd

defendant-appellant also had been returned undelivered with the

endorsement that he cannot be traced at the given address.

The 2nd defendant-appellant had failed to deposit the brief

fees. Therefore this Court is of the view that the appellants are not

Appellant has been issued with notice directing the

payment of brief fees. The letter addressed to him has been

returned with the endorsement that there is no house named

"Suramya" at Koswatta and in any event no person by that name

resides at Koswatta. As the registry has complied with the rule

by issuing the notice calling for the deposit of brief fees addressed

to the appellant at the address furnished by him, this appeal now

stands rejected in terms of Rule 13(b) of the preparation of appeal

brief-Court of Appeal - 1978.

K. T. CHITRASIRI, J.

Learned Counsel for the defendant-appellant referring to the

authorities in relation to Muhammadan Law submits that he is not in a

position to proceed with this appeal. He further submits that the

defendant had been living at this premises for 50 odd years and they

are still living in that premises.

Having considered those circumstances, Counsel for the plaintiff-

respondent agrees not to execute the writ for a period of one year

I

commencing from today, allowing the defendant either to purchase the

property or for him to find another place.

In view of the said agreement Counsel for the appellant moves to

withdraw this appeal. Application for withdrawal is allowed.

Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed without cost.

Appeal dismissed.

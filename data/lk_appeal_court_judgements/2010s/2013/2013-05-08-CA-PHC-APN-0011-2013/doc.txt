Sunil Rajapakshe, J.

1

We have gone through the petitioner's petition and submissions

made by the both parties. In my view petitioner has established exceptional

circumstances to release the suspect on bail. Learned State Counsel does not

object to release the suspect on bail. Considering all these facts, I release the

suspect on bail.

Court directs the learned Magistrate of Homagama to release the

suspect namely Dissanayakalage Manjula Nishantha Dissanayake on bail

subject to the following condition.

• f 2

The suspect should furnish cash bail in a sum of Rupees Ten

Thousand (Rs. 10,000/-with two sureties acceptable to the

learned Magistrate of Homagama.

The Registrar of this Court is directed to forward a copy of this

order to the Registrar, Magistrate's Court of Homagama and to the Officer-in-

Charge, Police Station, Homagama.

Petitioner is entitled to a certified copy of this order on payment of

usual charges.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

A.W.A. Salam, J.

I agree.

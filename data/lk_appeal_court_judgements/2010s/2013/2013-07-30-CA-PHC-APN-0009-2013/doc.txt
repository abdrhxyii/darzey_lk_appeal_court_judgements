A.W.A.SALAM, J.

This is a revision application to have the sentence imposed on

one W.G.R. Wajira Kumara varied. He has not been made a party to

these proceedings. Further the application has been filed by the

petitioner on the basis that he is the father of the accused. The State

has raised a preliminary objection as to the right of the petitioner to

maintain this application by reason of the following judgments of this

Court.

(1) Senathilaka Vs. A.G. 1998- 3-SLR 290

(2) M.P.Piyaseeli Vs. A.G. CA PHC 18/2009

(3) M.M.Sonali Fernando Vs. A.G. CA APN 144/2007

t t , ;

i I I

2

As has been clearly laid down in the above cases that no one else, other

than the accused himself can maintain an application to vary the

sentence. We uphold the preliminary objection and dismiss the revision

application.

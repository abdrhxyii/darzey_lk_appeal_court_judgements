K.T. Chitrasiri, J.

Both Counsel made submissions on their respective

cases. Argument concluded.

At this stage Mr. Guruge moves to withdraw the

appeal provided the plaintiff-respondent waive the damages awarded

to the plaintiff-respondent by the learned District Judge in his

judgment dated 30.10.1998. Having considered the circumstances,

Counsel for the plaintiff-respondent agrees to waive the aforesaid

damages awarded to the plaintiff by the impugned judgment.

2 \

In view of the above agreement and submissions

made by both the counsel, the Court allows the application for

withdrawal and accordingly the appeal is dismissed. However,

having considered the submissions of the learned counsel for the

I

plaintiff-respondent the order to pay damages to the plaintiff by the

defendant is set aside.

Accordingly, the appeal IS dismissed but the

plaintiff-respondent is not entitled for the damages awarded in the

judgment dated 30.10.1998.

Subject to the above variation, the appeal is dismissed.

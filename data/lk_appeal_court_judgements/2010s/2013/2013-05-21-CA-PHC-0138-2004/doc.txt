A.W.A Salam, I.

Learned State Counsel heard in support of the objection regarding

the maintainability of the appeal. This is an appeal preferred against the

judgment of the High Court which exercised appellate jurisdiction over

the conviction and sentence imposed on the accused for violating certain

provisions of the Penal Code. Based on the Judgment in Wickremesekera

Vs. OIC Ampara 2004 SLR volume one page 257, I am of the opinion

that this appeal cannot be maintained and if at all an appeal from the

judgment of the High Court has to be made to the Supreme Court with

the leave of that Court first had and obtained. In the circumstances, this

appeal stands dismissed.

Sunil Rajapakshe, J,

I agree

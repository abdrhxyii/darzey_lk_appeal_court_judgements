Counsel for the accused-appellant moves to have this appeal withdrawn. Counsel for

the accused-appellant concedes that no appeal is available to this Court against the impugned

judgment of the learned High Court Judge who had exercised appellate jurisdiction over a

conviction entered by the learned Magistrate. As this appeal cannot be maintained in terms of

the judgment in Officer-in-Charge Amparai Vs Wickramasekera 2004(1} SLR 257, this appeal

stands dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.

Sunil Rajapakshe, J

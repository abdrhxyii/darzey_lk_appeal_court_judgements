Heard both Counsel in support of their respective cases.

1

This is a Habeas Corpus Application filed by the petitioner

moving this Court to direct the respondents to produce the corpus in

Court. The petitioner alleges that on 10.03.2012 around 12.30 a.m.

four people claiming to be the police officers came and arrested her

husband at her house at Mattakkuliya. Soon after the arrest he had

been taken in a white van. The petitioner thereafter saw a police vehicle

parked little away from her house and thereupon went and complained

to the officers in the said vehicle. On their instructions the petitioner

made a complaint to the Modara Police Station in the same morning.

The respondents have denied the arrest of the corpus. When we consider

the material submitted by both parties, We are of the opinion that Court

2

should order the Magistrate to hold an inquiry. We direct the

Magistrate of Colombo to hold an inquiry on the petition filed by the

petitioner in this Court. Registrar of this Court is directed to send a

certified copy of the entire record to the Magistrate's Court. Petitioner is

at liberty to move this Court by way of a motion after the conclusion of

the inquiry by the Magistrate.

JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

P.W.D.C. JAYATHILAKA, J.

I agree.

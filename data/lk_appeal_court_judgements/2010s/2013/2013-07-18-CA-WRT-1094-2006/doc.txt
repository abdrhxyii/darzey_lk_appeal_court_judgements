Learned Counsel for the petitioner brings to the notice of Court that he has no instructions

from his client and he is unable to locate the whereabouts of his client. The learned Counsel also

submits to Court that the learned President's Counsel who appears for the petitioner has endeavoured

to dispatch a letter to his client, the petitioner. However there has been no response to same.

Perusing the caption the address stated therein the residence of Petitioner is in Hong Kong.

This is a 2006 writ application. These are discretionary remedies of Court and by this application Writ

of Mandamus and Certiorari/Prohibition has been sought. lt appears to this Court that the petitioner

is no longer interested in pursuing this application. These being discretionary remedies of Court, this

..

Court has no alternative but to reject the application of the petitioner. Application is dismissed. No

costs.

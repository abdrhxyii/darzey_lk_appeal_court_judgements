Learned President's Counsel appearing for the petitioner moves to amend the

caption by adding the present incumbent of the office of Director General of

Customs Mr. Jagath P. Wijeweera. Application to add the respondent is

allowed. Amended caption filed of record. Learned State Counsel takes notice

on behalf of him and in view of the administrative settlement, Counsel for the

petitioner supports the motion dated 11.07.2013 and informs court that both

parties agree that this matter be settled administratively and in view of the

administrative settlement the counsel for the petitioner moves to withdraw this

application. Application for withdrawal is allowed and this application is pro

forma dismissed without costs.

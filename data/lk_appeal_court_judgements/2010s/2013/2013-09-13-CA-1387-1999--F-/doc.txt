The plaintiff-appellant is absent and unrepresented.

The plaintiff-appellant is dead in this case and Court had

issued notices on the heirs of the deceased plaintiff-appellant. Notice

had been served on M.R. Somawathi, party sought to be substituted.

heirs of the deceased plaintiff-appellant is not interested in prosecuting

this appeal. Therefore, this Court makes order abating this appeal.

Appeal abated.

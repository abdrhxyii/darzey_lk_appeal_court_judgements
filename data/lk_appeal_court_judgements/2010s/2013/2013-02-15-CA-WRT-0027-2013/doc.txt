CA27-2013

Heard learned counsel for petitioner in support of this application. The

petitioner in this application is praying for a writ of certiorari to quash a

decision contained in P22. The decision contained in P22 is in relation to a

check point of the Sri Lanka Army which restricted the movement of people

through that point. The petitioner contended that those are the points

through wish the public have access to their shops and therefore they are not

in a position to do business in their shops allocated. The petitioner also

submits that the said premises which were allocated to the petitioner were

not put in use because of the fact that these check points were in existence

for some time and therefore the public has no access to the said premises.

The Ministry of Defence and Urban Development has requested the Sri

Lanka Army to consider whether they could pay rent to these buildings or to

allow access to the public. The relief sought in this application is based

on facts and not on questions of law and therefore this court cannot issue

writs in the nature that was sought in this application and therefore this court

refuses to issue notice.

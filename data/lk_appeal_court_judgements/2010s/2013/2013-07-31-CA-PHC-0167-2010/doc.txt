A.W.A.SALAM, J.

This appeal has been preferred against the judgment of the High

Court pronounced in the exercise of appellate jurisdiction over an order

made by the Magistrate. As such, this Court has no jurisdiction to

en tertain this appeal. Following the ratio decidendi in the case of

Wickramasekera Vs. OJ.C. Police Station, Ampara reported in 2004 1

SLR 257, we proceed to dismiss the appeal for want of jurisdiction.

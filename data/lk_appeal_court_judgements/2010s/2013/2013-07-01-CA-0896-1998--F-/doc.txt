K.T. CHITRASIRI, J.

This is an appeal to have a re-trial and to obtain reliefs similar

to the reliefs prayed for in the plaint. Significantly, no relief in the

Petition of Appeal is found seeking to set-aside the judgment. However,

upon perusal of the brief it is evident that the judgment that is being

challenged had been delivered on 09.0 1.1996. This appeal had been

filed on the 01.09.1998. The notice of appeal is dated 10.07.1998.

Accordingly, it is clear that this appeal had been filed more than two

years after the delivery of the impugned judgment. Counsel for the

appellant concedes this position. Therefore, this appeal cannot be

sustained. Accordingly the appeal is dismissed without cost.

Appeal dismissed.

A.W.A.Salam J.

The Appellant has been issued with notice requiring the payment of

brief fees, on or before 27.08.2013. He has failed to pay the brief fees.

The notice issued on him has been returned with the remark that he

has left the place.

As the registry has complied with the Rules by sending out the notice

to him at the address mentioned in the caption, we are of the view

that the failure on the part of the Appellant to pay brief fees should

culminate in this appeal being rejected under 13 (b). Accordingly this

appeal is rejected.

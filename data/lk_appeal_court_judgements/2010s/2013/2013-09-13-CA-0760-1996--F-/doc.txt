When this matter was mentioned on 30.08.2013 the

plaintiff-respondent appeared in person and informed Court that the

defendant-appellant had not complied with the terms of settlement

dated 24.02.2010. Accordingly, Court requested the plaintiff-respondent

to support his application with legal assistance today. According to the

terms of settlement entered on 24.02.2010 before this Court, the

defendant-appellant had agreed to pay the plaintiff-respondent a sum of

2 i

Rs.105,000/= as full and final settlement on or before 28.09.2010.

Accordingly, the Court had allowed the appeal to be withdrawn by the

defendant-appellant. It was further agreed that if the defendant-

appellant fails to make the said payment on or before 28.09.2010 the

plaintiff-respondent is entitled to apply for a writ of execution to recover

the balance money owed by the defendant-appellant to him. Therefore,

as the appeal has been withdrawn by the defendant-appellant the

Registrar is directed to send a copy of this order together with the order

made by this Court on 24.02.2010 and the original case record to the

District Court of KUliyapitiya.

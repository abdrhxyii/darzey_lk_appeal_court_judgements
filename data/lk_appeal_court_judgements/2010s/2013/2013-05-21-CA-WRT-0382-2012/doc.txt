Learned Senior State Counsel submitted to court that the Pension

Department has agreed to pay the Widows and Orphans Pension to the

Petitioner from 28.11.1989 the date on which her husband's demise

demised and up to 16.08.2010. This payment was under Section 30 of

the Widows and Orphans Pension as amended by Act No: 44 of 1981.

The Petitioner's pension had been paid from 17.08.2010. In view of

this undertaking the counsel for the Petitioner moves to withdraw this

application. Application for withdrawal is allowed and this application

is pro-forma dismissed without costs.

Application dismissed.

Rohini Marasinghe, J.

This is an appeal filed by the State against the judgment of the High

Courtjudge dated 29.07.2009.

The facts of the Petition are briefly as follows.

Petitioner had made an application to the High Court of Kalmunai

seeking a writ of certiorari to quash orders contain in documents marked

as P3 and P3A. However, now the Court is informed that the Petitioner

had retired from service. In those circumstances the State inform that

they do not intend to pursue this appeal. Therefore, Counsel for the State

moves to withdraw this appeal. Application for withdrawal is allowed.

Registrar is directed to inform the relevant Court the appeal has been

dismissed.

Deepali Wijesundera, J.

I agree.

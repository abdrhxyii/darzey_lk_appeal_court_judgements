Learned State Counsel is heard on the respondent.

1

This is an appeal preferred against the judgment of the

learned High Court Judge delivered in the exercise of the original

jurisdiction under Article 154(P)(3) of the Constitution. The appellant

has sought a writ of mandamus in respect of a State land governed by

Land Development Ordinance. The learned High Court Judge has refused

to issue the writ inter alia that the subject matter constitutes a land

belonging to the State. In the circumstances we do not see any reason

2

to vary the findings and the judgment of the learned High Court Judge

and therefore dismiss this appeal. There shall be no costs.

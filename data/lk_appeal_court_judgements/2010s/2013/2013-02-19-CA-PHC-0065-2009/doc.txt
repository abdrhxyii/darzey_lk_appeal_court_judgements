Rohini Marasinghe, J.

The appellant in this case has made an application seeking inter alia a writ to

quash the documents marked as P5A and P7A.

According to the submissions made by Counsel for the appellant the

decisions contained/and or facts contained in the impugned documents

marked as P5A and P7 A have now been rectified.

Therefore, as the documents P5A and P7 A have now been amended the

appellant moves to withdraw the appeal receiving the right to re agitate the

appeal if necessary.

The application is allowed. The appeal is withdrawn subject to the condition

mentioned.

Proceedings terminated.

Deepali Wijesundera, J.

I agree.

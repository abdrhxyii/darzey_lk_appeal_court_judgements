Anil Gooneratne.J.

Counsel for the petitioner informs Court that she has no instructions from her client

namely the three petitioners.

Counsel also informs Court that he intends to revoke the proxy and would file a proper

motion in the registry in due course. This is a 2009 application. Originally this matter came up before

this Court on 26.10.2009. Since then this application has been listed for hearing at least on about 14

occasions on the last date (02.04.2013) Counsel appeared for the petitioner. However the position is

different today. Since counsel has informed Court that he has no instructions from the client, it

appears to this Court that the petitioners are no longer interested in pursuing this application. These

are discretionary remedies of Court. We see no basis have this case on the role. As such the

application is dismissed. No costs.

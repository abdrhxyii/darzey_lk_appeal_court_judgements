Upaly Abeyrathne, J

According to the fiscal report the appellant was not at the given address. Learned

counsel for the respondent informs Court that his client has come to know that the appellant has

died. Heirs of the appellant has failed to file papers for substitution. I have considered the said

circumstances. It seems that the heirs of the appellant has not shown due diligence in taking

necessary steps for the purpose of prosecuting this appeal. In the said circumstances I dismiss the

appeal of the appellant without costs.

Appeal dismissed.

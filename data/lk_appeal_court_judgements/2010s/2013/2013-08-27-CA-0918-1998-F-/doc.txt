K.T. Chitrasiri. J

Consequent to the preparation of briefs, Registrar has sent

notices to the parties as well as to their Attorneys-at-Law. The notice

sent to the 2nd defendant-appellant has not been returned. Notice sent

to his Attorney-at-Law had been returned with the endorsement that

there is no such person. The notice sent to the plaintiff-respondent had

been returned with the endorsement that he has left the place. Notice

sent to the 1st and 3rd defendant-respondents had not been returned. All

those notices have been sent to the addresses given in the petition of

appeal and in the notice of appeal. Those are the addresses given even

in the plaint filed in the District Court of Matugama. It is seen that the

parties are not before Court, despite all attempts have been taken to

inform them of the date of hearing of this appeal. Hence, this appeal

cannot be taken up for argument. Accordingly Court makes an order

abating the appeal.

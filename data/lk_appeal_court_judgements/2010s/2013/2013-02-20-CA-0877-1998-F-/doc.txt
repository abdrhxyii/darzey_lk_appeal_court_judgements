Dhanasha Kaneshayogam for the 4th Defendant-Appellant.

20.02.2013.

Mr. N. Bathalameous who is watching the interest of the heirs of

the deceased plaintiff-respondent submits that his clients have not been

made parties to this appeal even though they were substituted in the

District Court. He also submits that both notices of appeal filed by the

1st and 2nd defendant-appellants and the 4th defendant had been filed on

30 I 7 I 1998 and therefore both notices were filed out side the period of

time, allowed under Section 755 of the Civil Procedure Code. Having

perused the original record, Counsel for the 4th Defendant-Appellant

concedes that both the notices of appeal had been filed out of time.

Upon perusal of both notices of appeals, it is clear that the notices

had been tendered on 30 I 7 I 1998 and it is clearly visible on the two

notices. Furthermore, two journal entries made on 131711998 which

are found at page 31 of the brief also confirm this position.

•

2

In view of the above circumstances, Court decides that the notices

of appeal of the 1st and 2nd defendant-appellants and the 4th defendant

appellant have been filed after 14 days from the date of the judgment

that falls out side the period allowed in terms of Section 755 of the Civil

Procedure Code. Accordingly, the appeal of the 4th defendant-appellant

is dismissed. There is no appeal filed by the 1st and 2nd defendants.

Hence, the notice of appeal filed by the 1st and 2nd defendants are

rejected.

Registrar is directed to send the original record back to the District

Court of Puttlam after keeping the record in this Court for a period of two

months. No costs.

Appeal dismissed.

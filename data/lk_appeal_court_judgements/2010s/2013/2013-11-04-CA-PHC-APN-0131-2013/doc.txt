Heard Counsel in support of the reVISIOn application. Learned

Senior State Counsel expressed her willingness to abide by any order, the

Court makes on the matter. Having perused the impugned order we do

not see any reason warranting the cancellation of bail by the learned

High Court Judge and therefore we set aside the order of the learned

High Court Judge dated 30.05.2013. The Accused will be on the same

bail conditions as stated in the order of the Court of Appeal in C.A.

476/2005 minute dated 14.09.2005 except the condition that he will

have to report to the Registrar of the respective Magistrate's Court on the

last Friday of every month before 9.00 a.m. to 3.00 p.m. until the case is

concluded instead of having to report to the Officer -in-Charge of the

Police station as stated to be bail condition in the said order of the Court

of Appeal in C.A. 476/2005

01.02.2013

The Registrar of this Court has issued notices on the defendant-appellant

as well as to his Registered Attorney on several occasions directing them to be

present in this Court to proceed with this appeal. In the journal entry made on

05.11.12, it is stated that the notices sent to the defendant-appellant had been

returned with an endorsement that he has passed away. Notice sent to the

Registered Attorney had also been returned with an endorsement that he has left

the country.

In the circumstances, this Court is unable to proceed with this appeal.

Accordingly, Court makes an order abating the appeal.

1

Appeal abated and accordingly the proceedings in this Court are

terminated.

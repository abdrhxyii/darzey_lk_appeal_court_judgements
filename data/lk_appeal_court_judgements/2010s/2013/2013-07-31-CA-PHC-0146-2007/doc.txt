A.W.A.SALAM, J.

Respondent-petitioner-appellant absent and unrepresented

despite notice having been issued requiring the payment of brief fees. Notice

issued on the appellant has been returned with the endorsement that the

appellant has left the address. The appellant has failed to keep the

Registrar informed of any change of address and the issuance of notice at

the address given in the caption is sufficient compliance of the

Rules by the Registrar. As such for failure on the part of the appellant to

deposit brief fees, this appeal is rejected.

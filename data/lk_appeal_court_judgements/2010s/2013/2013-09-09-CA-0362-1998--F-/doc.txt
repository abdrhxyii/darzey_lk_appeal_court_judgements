K.T. CHITRASIRI,J.

Consequent to the preparation of briefs, the Registrar of this Court has sent

notices to the Appellant and to her Attorney-at-Law as well as to the Defendant-

Respondent. Neither of them are present in Court nor are they being represented by an

Attorney-at-Law. Those notices had been sent under registered cover. Notice sent to

the Plaintiff-Appellant had been returned with the endorsement that there is no such

person at the given address. When this matter was mentioned on 28.07.2011, Counsel

for the Defendant-Respondent had informed Court that both the Plaintiff-Appellant and

her Registered Attorney are dead. In those circumstances, this appeal cannot be taken

up for hearing.

In the circumstances, this Court makes an order abating the appeal. The Registrar

is directed to send the original record back to the District Court of Matara.

Appeal is abated.

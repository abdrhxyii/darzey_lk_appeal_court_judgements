This is an appeal preferred against the judgment of the learned

High Court Judge, refusing to entertain the application filed in revision of

the order of the learned Magistrate confiscating the vehicle in question. The

revision application has been dismissed inter alia on the following grounds:

1) That the affidavit tendered along with the petition has not been

sworned or affirmed to by the Chairman or Director of the Company.

2) That the petitioner has failed to disclose exceptional circumstances

warranting the exercise of revisionary powers.

As regards the purported defects pointed out by the learned High

Court Judge it has to be observed that the affidavit tendered in support of

the averments in the petition need not be necessarily by the Chairman or

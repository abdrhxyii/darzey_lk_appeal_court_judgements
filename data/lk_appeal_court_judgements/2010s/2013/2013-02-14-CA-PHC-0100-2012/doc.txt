Rohini Marasinghe, J.

Counsel for the Petitioner submits that the accused in this case had been

taken into custody on 18.04.2010 on allegation of robbery. However the

basis of the application of the Counsel for the Petitioner is that this

suspect had been on remand for more than 3 years without any charge

or trial. Contrary to these submissions Counsel for the State submits that

this accused has several pending cases for offences of similar nature and

the present case involves robbery of Rs. 71 lakhs. Counsel for the State

further submits that the suspect has been identified at the parade. Most

importantly no person should be kept on remand for such a long period

of time without being charged. If the investigation had not been

completed the State must take steps to have the suspect released until

there is sufficient material to charge the suspect. No person cannot be

I

I ..

kept on remand if investigations are not completed which means there is

no evidence against this suspect up to now. Therefore, we reject the

submissions of the Counsel for the State and direct the accused to be

released on Rs. 50,000/- cash bail, in addition two responsible

government servants as sureties. We direct the Magistrate of

Kaduwela to release this suspect on bail on the aforesaid direction.

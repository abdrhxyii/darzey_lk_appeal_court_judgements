Heard both counsel in support of their respective cases.

The accused-appellant in this case was convicted of the murder of his

own child named Amarasinghege Visandika and was sentenced to death.

Six year old child of the accused-appellant has given evidence and stated

that the accused-appellant took the child who was on the floor and dashed

her on the floor. Accused-appellant again dashed the child on the floor. In

his dock statement he has admitted that when he was lifting the child, she

fell on the ground. But according to the medical evidence there was a

depressed fracture. Learned trial Judge has considered his dock statement

and the evidence led at the trial and had come to the conclusion that the

dock statement cannot be believed. In our view the dock statement cannot

prosecution case. In these circumstances, we hold that the learned trial

Judge was right when he convicted the accused-appellant for the offence of

murder. We hold that there is no merit in this appeal and we affirm the

conviction and the sentence.

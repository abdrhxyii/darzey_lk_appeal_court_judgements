17th June, 2013

The defendant-appellant is present in Court.

1

Both Counsel inform Court that the dispute in this matter is

now settled between the parties on the following terms:-

1. The plaintiff- respondent agrees to transfer a portion of land in

extent of 20 perches to the defendant-appellant from the land in

dispute. The land in dispute is depicted in plan bearing No. An

1785 dated 01.06.2013 drawn by S. Mariyathas, Licensed

Surveyor.

2. The portion of the land in extent of 20 perches that is to be

transferred to the defendant-appellant, is to be demarcated

bordering the Southern boundary of the land referred to in the

said plan 1785.

2

3. The defendant-appellant agrees to bear the expenses for the survey

to be carried out in order to demarcate the land to be transferred

to him. Expenses to be incurred even for the execution of the deed

to be borne by the defendant-appellant.

4. The plaintiff -respondent agrees to allow the defendant-appellant to

remove the building marked "P", in the aforesaid plan No. An 1785

without any compensation being paid to the plaintiff-respondent.

In view of the above settlement, the defendant-appellant

moves to withdraw this appeal. The application for withdrawal of the

appeal is allowed. Accordingly the appeal is dismissed subject to the

above terms of settlement arrived at in this Court by the parties to the

action.

Appeal dismissed.

Notice issued on the appellant calling for brief fees has been

returned with the remark "address insufficient". The letter addressed

to the appellant has been directed to be served at the address furnished

by the appellant. Since the appellant has not furnished any alternative

address and the Registrar has complied with the Rule by sending

notices to the given address, for failure to deposit brief fees, this appeal

is rejected.

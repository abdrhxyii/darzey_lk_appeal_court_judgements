19.02.2013

When this matter was mentioned on 26.06.2012, it was brought to the notice

of court that the defendant - appellant has expired. Counsel for the

respondent also confirms this fact. The Registrar of this court has sent a

notice to the registered attorney of the defendant-appellant as well,

informing him that this matter would be taken up for argument today. He is

also not present in court.

It must be noted that this appeal cannot be taken up for argument without

substituting the heirs of the appellant in the room of the deceased defendant-

appellant. Neither the heirs of the appellant nor the registered attorney of

the appellant is taking steps to substitute the heirs of the defendant-

appellant in the room of the deceased defendant. Accordingly court makes

an order abating the appeal.

•

Registrar is directed to send the original record back to the District Court of

Galle enabling the learned District Judge to take necessary steps, having

kept the original record for two months in this court. In view of the above

order made, abating the appeal, proceedings in this court are terminated.

Proceedings terminated.

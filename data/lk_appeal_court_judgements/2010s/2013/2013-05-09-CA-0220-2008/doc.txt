Learned Counsel appearing for the accused-appellant makes an application

to withdraw the appeal. Application for withdrawal of the appeal is allowed

and the appeal is dismissed.

She further makes an application to implement the sentence from the date of

sentencing by the learned High Court Judge of Baticaloa. Learned ASG has

no objection for this application.

Both Counsel submit that the accused -appellant, after conviction, has not

been released on bail. We, acting under 359 of the Criminal Procedure Code,

direct the Prison Authorities to implement the sentence from the date of

sentencing by the learned High Court Judge (14.03.2008)

Appeal dismissed.

Sisira De Abrew, J.

Learned Counsel for the Accused-Appellant after arguing the

case makes an application to withdraw the appeal. Application for

withdrawal of the appeal is allowed. Appeal is dismissed.

This is a case of rape. The facts of this case may be briefly

summarized as follows:

The accused-appellant, around 5.00 p.m. on the day of the

incident, came to the house of the prosecutrix looking for her

husband. When he found that the husband was not at home he

dragged the prosecutrix to a room in the house and raped her. At

the time of the incident he was armed with a knife. According to

the prosecutrix, the accused-appellant, on seeing her husband

coming, fled from the place. Before he did so he threw Rs. 200/-

to the table of the house. Soon after the incident the prosecutrix

complained the incident to her husband and thereupon both of

them went and lodged a complaint to the police station. The

accused-appellant denied the incident but admitted that at the

time of rape he came to the house of the prosecutrix. When we

consider the facts of this case, we are of the opinion that the

prosecution has proved the case beyond reasonable doubt.

Therefore the conviction of the accused appellant by the learned

High Court Judge is, in our opinion, right. We affirm the

conviction and the sentence. We direct the Prison Authorities to

implement the sentence from the date of this judgment (today).

Appeal dismissed.

1

Counsel for the petitioner and the learned Senior State Counsel

were heard in support of the application.

Learned Senior State Counsel has no objection to the suspect,

Y akgahahewage Chandana Wickremasinghe being enlarged on bail on

appropriate conditions. We considered the fact that the suspect on whose

behalf the application for bail has been made in the High Court was on

remand for nearly one year and nine months and the period of further

extension sought by the Attorney General also has expired. Therefore, we

decide to set aside the order of the learned High Court Judge refusing bail

and substitute the same with the following bail conditions.

2

1. The suspect, Y akgahahewage Chandana Wickremasinghe to be

released on cash bail in a sum of Rs.lOO,OOO /=( One Hundred

Thousand Rupees) with 04 sureties acceptable to the learned

Magistrate. The quantum of surety bail is Rs.500,000/= each.

2. As a condition precedent to the suspect to be released on bail, he

should hand over his passport to Court, which passport to be

impounded by the learned Magistrate and inform the relevant

Authorities.

The registrar is directed to forward a copy of this order to the

Magistrate's Court of Balapitiya.

The certified copy of this order to be issued to the petitioner on

payment of usual charges.

A.W.A.Salam, J.

Notice issued on the appellant has been returned with the

endorsement that he is unable to be traced at the given address. As

the appellant has furnished only one address, Registry has no

alternative but to address the notice to the given address. Even if

notice is reissued it will not serve any useful purpose. This appeal is

now rejected for failure to deposit brief fees, subject to the appellant

making an application for relisting if advised to do so. The Registrar is

directed to send a copy of this judgment by registered post to the

appellant for his information.

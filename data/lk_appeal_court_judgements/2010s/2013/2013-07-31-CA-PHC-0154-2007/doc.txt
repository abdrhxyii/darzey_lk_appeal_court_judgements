A.W.A.SALAM. J.

Appellants absent and unrepresented. Notices have been issued

on the appellants calling for the payment of brief fees. The notice issued on

the 2nd appellant has not been returned whilst the notice issued on the 1st

appellant has been returned with the endorsement that he has left the

address. The 2nd appellant has failed to notify any change of address and

therefore despatching the notice at the address given in the caption is

sufficient compliance of the Rules. Hence, for failure on the part of the

appellants to deposit the brief fees, this appeal is rejected.

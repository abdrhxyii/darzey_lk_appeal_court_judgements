04.04.2013.

When this matter was mentioned on the last occasion

namely on the 11.02.2013, it was brought to the notice of Court

that the substituted defendant-respondent had died on 10.4.2007.

Thereafter the appellant had moved Court to file substitution

papers despite the fact that he was given several dates to file

substitution papers even before. On the last occasion it was

recorded that the substitution papers should be filed two weeks

before today. So far no substitution papers have been filed to

effect the substitution in place of the deceased substituted

defendant-respondent. For the last 6 years, no steps have been

taken to substitute the heirs of the deceased substituted-

1

defendant-respondent. Those circumstances show that the

appellant is not prosecuting this appeal with due diligence.

Accordingly this appeal is abated.

In view of the above order , proceedings in this Court

are terminated.

Proceedings terminated.

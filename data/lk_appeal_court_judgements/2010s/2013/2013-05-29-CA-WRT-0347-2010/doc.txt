S. Sriskandarajah, J. (PICA)

Learned DSG brings it to the notice of Court that the 2nd Petitioner who is

seeking a Mandamus from this Court to direct the Respondent to permit the

retirement of the Petitioner is now being granted by the Public Service

Commission. This was communicated by letter dated 02.08.2012 addressed to

the secretary to the Land Ministry, the retirement was with effect from

02.01.2012. The 1st Petitioner is also seeking the same relief, the 1st Petitioner~

request was pending before Public Service Commission. The Public Service

Commission has yet to make a decision. In view of this, this court cannot

consider this application in view of the Provisions contained in Article 61 (a) of

the constitution. In view of the above this application is pro-forma dismissed

without costs.

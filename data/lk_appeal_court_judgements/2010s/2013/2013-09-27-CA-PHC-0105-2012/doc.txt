DECIDED ON: 27.09.2013

A.W.A. Salam, J

This is an appeal preferred against the judgment of the learned High

Court Judge dismissing the appeal preferred by the Respondent-

Appellant-Appellant against the order of the learned Magistrate

directing the enforcement of the certificate to recover the gratuity

due to an employee. The learned High Court Judge has dismissed

the appeal on the strength of the ratio in the judgment of this court

in appeal No CA(PHC)APN 299/03 on the premise that no appeal

filed against the order of the learned Magistrate under the

Provisions of Act No 12 of 1993. As such, we see no reason to defer

from the opinion held by the learned High Court Judge as to the

maintainability of the appeal had in the Provincial High Court.

Consequently, this appeal stands dismissed.

P.W.D.C. Jayathilake, J.

I agree.

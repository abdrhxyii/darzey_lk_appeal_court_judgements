26.06.2015.

Counsel for the Accused appellant informs Court that he has no

compelling grounds to challenge the conviction and sentence in this

case imposed on the accused appellant by the learned High Court

Judge. Counsel submits that the victim of this case had been 6 years

at the time the commitment of the offence. The medical evidence

shows that she had been severely injured by the acts of the accused

appellant. The medical evidence corroborates the prosecution version.

Counsel for the Respondent submits that at this stage there is

enough evidence to convict the Accused AJ&pellant on the charges and

there is no reason to interfere with. the said conviction and the

sentence imposed by the learned High Court Judge. We have perused

the evidence led in this case and are of the view that there is sufficient

evidence to convict the Accused Appellant.

We are also of the view that the dock statement of the Accused

considering all the submissions and the evidence in this case we

dismiss the appeal of the Accused Appellant confirming the conviction

and the sentence of the learned High Court Judge on 06.02.2014.

Appeal dismissed.

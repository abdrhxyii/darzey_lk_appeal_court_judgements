At this stage counsel for the Accused -Appellant informs Court that

she will confine this appeal to the sentence imposed by the learned High

Court judge on the Accused-Appellant. She submits to Court that the

Accused-Appellant was only 19 years at the time of the commission of

the offence and further he has been languishing in remand for a period of

five years due to the fact that there was no facilities in this Court to

translate the said Tamil brief in to English language. Counsel for the

Respondent too concedes these facts. Counsel also moves this court to

consider reducing the amount of compensation ordered by the learned

High Court Judge, that is to pay five lakhs to the victim of this case and

states that the Accused has no capacity to pay the amount stated

therein.

1

•

Counsel for the Accused-Appellant moves to Court to act under

Section 359 of the Criminal procedure Code and to make an order for the

sentence to be implemented from the date of conviction.

Counsel for the Respondent does not object to the said application.

Therefore, after considering facts and submissions made by the

counsel we reduce the compensation ordered to the victim of this case to

two lakhs and in default a period of one year Rigorous imprisonment.

We also direct that the said sentence imposed on of the Accused-

Appellant be implemented from the date of conviction namely

16.09.2009. Subject to the variation of the sentence appeal is dismissed.

Appeal dismissed.
